Front End Developer

Are you a talented and passionate Front End Developer looking to work with a dynamic and creative team? Hot Beans, a leading web design company, is seeking a skilled professional to join our ranks. As a Front End Developer at Hot Beans, you will play a pivotal role in bringing our clients' visions to life through captivating and intuitive user interfaces.

Responsibilities

  • • Collaborate with designers and back-end developers to implement visually stunning and functional web designs.
  • • Translate design concepts into clean, efficient, and standards-compliant code.
  • • Ensure seamless user experiences across different devices and browsers.
  • • Optimize website performance for speed and responsiveness.
  • • Collaborate with designers and back-end developers to implement visually stunning and functional web designs.

Required Qualifications

  • • Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Web Development, or a related field (or equivalent experience).
  • • Proven experience in front end development, including HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.
  • • Proficiency in responsive web design and familiarity with CSS frameworks (e.g., Bootstrap, Foundation).
  • • Strong understanding of cross-browser compatibility and accessibility standards.
  • • Experience with version control systems (e.g., Git) and front end build tools (e.g., Gulp, Webpack).
  • • Excellent problem-solving skills and attention to detail.

Salary

£55-60k Per Annum (Depending On Experience)

We offer competitive compensation packages commensurate with experience and expertise. Rest assured, your hard work and dedication will be rewarded.
