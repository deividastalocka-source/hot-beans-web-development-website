DevOps Engineer

Are you a talented and experienced DevOps Engineer passionate about streamlining development processes and optimizing system performance? Look no further! Hot Beans, a leading web design company, is seeking a skilled DevOps Engineer to join our cutting-edge team. As a DevOps Engineer at Hot Beans, you will play a vital role in enhancing our development workflow and ensuring efficient and scalable operations.

Responsibilities

  • • Collaborate with development and operations teams to establish effective deployment pipelines and automate software releases.
  • • Implement and maintain CI/CD processes for efficient software delivery.
  • • Monitor and optimize system performance, scalability, and reliability.
  • • Establish and maintain infrastructure as code using tools like Terraform or Ansible.
  • • Implement security measures and ensure compliance with best practices.
  • • Troubleshoot and resolve issues related to infrastructure, deployment, and system performance.

Required Qualifications

  • • Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or a related field (or equivalent experience).
  • • Proven experience in DevOps, system administration, or related roles.
  • • Strong understanding of software development lifecycle and experience with CI/CD tools (e.g., Jenkins, GitLab CI/CD).
  • • Proficiency in scripting and automation using languages like Bash, Python, or PowerShell.
  • • Experience with containerization technologies like Docker and container orchestration tools like Kubernetes.
  • • Familiarity with cloud platforms such as AWS, Azure, or Google Cloud.
  • • Knowledge of infrastructure as code tools (e.g., Terraform, Ansible) and configuration management systems.
  • • Strong problem-solving and communication skills.

Salary

£65-90k Per Annum (Depending On Experience)

Hot Beans values the expertise and contributions of skilled DevOps Engineers. We offer competitive salary packages that reflect your skills, expertise, and industry experience. Our salary offerings are designed to attract top talent and reward your commitment and innovation. In addition to competitive compensation, we provide opportunities for professional growth and continuous learning within our dynamic organization.
