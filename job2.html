Back End Developer

Are you a skilled and driven Back End Developer seeking a challenging role within an innovative company? Look no further! Hot Beans, a leading web design company, is searching for a talented Back End Developer to join our team. As a Back End Developer at Hot Beans, you will play a crucial role in developing robust and scalable web applications that power our clients' digital experiences.

Responsibilities

  • • Collaborate with front end developers and designers to architect and build efficient and reliable server-side systems.
  • • Develop APIs and integrate third-party services for seamless data exchange.
  • • Implement secure authentication and authorization mechanisms.
  • • Optimize database queries and ensure efficient data storage and retrieval.
  • • Conduct thorough testing and debugging to ensure smooth functionality and error-free code.

Required Qualifications

  • • Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or a related field (or equivalent experience).
  • • Strong proficiency in at least one back end programming language, such as Python, Java, or Ruby.
  • • Experience with back end frameworks and technologies, such as Django, Node.js, or Ruby on Rails.
  • • Solid understanding of database management systems (e.g., MySQL, PostgreSQL) and data modeling.
  • • Familiarity with RESTful API design principles and implementation.
  • • Knowledge of version control systems (e.g., Git) and proficiency in writing clean, maintainable code.
  • • Excellent problem-solving skills and attention to detail.

Salary

£75-90k Per Annum (Depending On Experience)

We value the contributions of our skilled team members and believe in providing competitive compensation packages. As a Back End Developer at Hot Beans, you can expect a salary that aligns with industry standards and rewards your expertise and experience. Our salary packages are designed to recognize and retain top talent, while also offering opportunities for growth and advancement within our organization. Join us and become a vital part of a team that values your skills and invests in your professional development.
