Full Stack Developer

Are you a versatile and skilled Full Stack Developer looking for an opportunity to work on exciting and diverse projects? Hot Beans, a leading web design company, is seeking a talented Full Stack Developer to join our dynamic team. As a Full Stack Developer at Hot Beans, you will have the chance to contribute to both the front end and back end aspects of our web development projects, delivering comprehensive and seamless digital solutions.

Responsibilities

  • • Develop and maintain both client-side and server-side applications.
  • • Collaborate with designers and stakeholders to translate requirements into functional and visually appealing user interfaces
  • • Implement robust and efficient APIs for seamless data integration.
  • • Optimize website performance and ensure responsive design across various devices.
  • • Conduct thorough testing and debugging to ensure high-quality and error-free code.

Required Qualifications

  • • Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or a related field (or equivalent experience).
  • • Proficiency in front end technologies such as HTML, CSS, and JavaScript..
  • • Experience with front end frameworks like React, Angular, or Vue.js.
  • • Strong knowledge of at least one back end programming language, such as Python, Java, or Ruby.
  • • Familiarity with back end frameworks like Node.js, Django, or Ruby on Rails.
  • • Understanding of database management systems and proficiency in SQL.
  • • Experience with version control systems and deployment tools.
  • • Excellent problem-solving skills and ability to work in a collaborative team environment.

Salary

£75-90k Per Annum (Depending On Experience)

At Hot Beans, we recognize the value of versatile and skilled Full Stack Developers, and we offer competitive salary packages to attract top talent. The salary for this position will be commensurate with your skills, expertise, and industry experience. We believe in rewarding our team members for their contributions, providing opportunities for growth, and fostering a positive work environment.
