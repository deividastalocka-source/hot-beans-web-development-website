UX / UI Designer

Are you a talented and imaginative UX/UI Designer seeking an opportunity to shape engaging digital experiences? Look no further! Hot Beans, a leading web design company, is searching for a skilled UX/UI Designer to join our creative team. As a UX/UI Designer at Hot Beans, you will play a pivotal role in crafting intuitive and visually captivating user interfaces that enhance user satisfaction and drive conversions.

Responsibilities

  • • Collaborate with cross-functional teams to understand project goals and user requirements.
  • • Create wireframes, prototypes, and visual designs that align with user-centered design principles.
  • • Conduct user research, interviews, and usability testing to inform design decisions.
  • • Iterate and refine designs based on feedback and data analysis.
  • • Collaborate with developers to ensure seamless implementation of design solutions.

Required Qualifications

  • • Bachelor's degree in Design, Human-Computer Interaction, or a related field (or equivalent experience).
  • • Proven experience in UX/UI design for web and mobile applications.
  • • Proficiency in design tools such as Sketch, Figma, or Adobe Creative Suite.
  • • Solid understanding of user-centered design principles and best practices.
  • • Experience with prototyping tools like InVision or Framer.
  • • Strong communication and collaboration skills.
  • • Attention to detail and a passion for delivering exceptional user experiences.

Salary

£65-90k Per Annum (Depending On Experience)

Hot Beans recognizes the immense value that talented UX/UI Designers bring to our projects. We offer competitive salary packages that reflect your skills, expertise, and industry experience. We believe in rewarding our team members for their contributions and providing opportunities for growth and professional development.

Join Hot Beans and be part of a creative team where your design skills will thrive. Apply now to embark on an exciting journey as a UX/UI Designer and shape impactful digital experiences for our clients.
